
# 🛒 Customer Shopping Behaviour  Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Customer Shopping Behaviour  Dashboard** is an end-to-end multi-tool data analytics project designed to analyze customer demographics, purchasing habits, spending patterns, product category preferences, payment choices, and subscription retention metrics.

The project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business intelligence covering total revenue, average order value (AOV), discount sensitivity, seasonal purchasing trends, and review rating distributions using **Python**, **SQL**, and **Microsoft Power BI**.

The repository features structured modules covering automated data preprocessing, relational SQL query models, dynamic Power BI visualizations, and clear folder-based project assets for seamless execution.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze purchasing behavior across customer demographic segments (age, gender, location).
- Track revenue generation and sales volume across primary product categories (Clothing, Footwear, Outerwear, Accessories).
- Evaluate customer payment preferences and shipping method adoption.
- Assess the business impact of discounts, promotional codes, and active subscriptions on order frequency and average spending.
- Deliver an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard equipped with dynamic visual slicers and page-to-page navigation.
- Craft optimized, production-ready SQL scripts for business aggregations, customer segmentation, and performance auditing.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python**: Data Extraction, Cleaning, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using Pandas & NumPy.
- **SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL / SQLite)**: Data Modeling, Window Functions, Aggregations, and CTEs.
- **Microsoft Power BI**: Interactive Visualizations, DAX Measures, and Data Relationships.

---

## 📁 Dataset & Modules

The dataset captures full-spectrum customer transaction profiles and purchasing metadata:

- **Customer Demographics**: Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status, and Previous Purchases Count.
- **Transaction Details**: Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount ($), Size, Color, Season.
- **Logistics & Sales**: Shipping Type, Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Payment Method.
- **Customer Feedback**: Review Ratings (1.0 to 5.0) and Purchase Frequency (Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Annually).

# 📊 Executive Business Insights: Customer Shopping Behavior

## 📈 Executive Summary KPIs
* **Total Customer Base:** **3,900** total transactions analyzed across the dataset.
* **Average Ticket Size:** **$59.76** spent per transaction.
* **Customer Satisfaction:** **3.75 out of 5.0** average product review rating, indicating overall positive sentiment with room for experience optimization.
* **Subscription Penetration Rate:** **27.0%** net active subscriber rate, leaving **73.0%** of customers non-subscribed.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

### 1. Subscription & Revenue Potential
* **Low Recurring Revenue Base:** Only **27%** of shoppers are subscribed, while **73%** make non-subscription purchases.
* **Business Opportunity:** Upselling non-subscribed shoppers into recurring membership models represents the largest growth lever to increase Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and retention.

### 2. Category Revenue Drivers vs. Sales Volume
* **Dominant Category:** **Clothing** is the top product driver by both metrics, generating **$104K** in revenue across ~1,730 total sales units.
* **Secondary Revenue Driver:** **Accessories** follows in second place, generating **$74K** in revenue with ~1,240 sales units.
* **Niche Categories:** **Footwear** ($36K revenue) and **Outerwear** ($19K revenue) account for smaller revenue shares, indicating lower product demand or catalog size.

### 3. Demographic & Age Group Breakdown
* **Top Revenue Segment:** **Young Adults** lead overall purchasing power, contributing approximately **$62K** in revenue across over 1,000 orders.
* **Consistent Spending Across Demographics:** Spending remains steady across **Middle-aged**, **Adult**, and **Senior** cohorts (~$55K to $58K each).
* **Volume vs. Spend Variance:** While **Seniors** generate slightly more total sales volume than **Adults**, **Adults** generate marginally higher total revenue, pointing to higher average purchase amounts among working-age adults.

## 🎯 Strategic Action Plan & Recommendations

1. **🚀 Launch Subscription Conversion Campaigns**
   * Target the **73% non-subscriber base** with incentives such as free express shipping, exclusive discounts, or reward points to boost the **27% subscription rate**.

2. **👕 Expand High-Margin Clothing & Accessories Lines**
   * Allocate inventory and marketing budget toward **Clothing ($104K)** and **Accessories ($74K)**, as they generate **76% of overall category revenue**.

3. **🎯 Tailor Campaigns for Young Adults**
   * Leverage social media channels and digital marketing aimed at **Young Adults**, who represent the highest revenue-generating demographic cohort.

4. **⭐ Improve Low Review Rating Products**
   * Analyze individual product feedback to raise the average rating from **3.75 toward 4.5+**, boosting customer trust and reducing return rates.
